# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing 50,000 e-commerce transactions using Excel, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and geographical trends.

The objective is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights using data cleaning, SQL analysis, KPI tracking, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue but had a comparatively lower profit margin.
- Fashion achieved the highest category-level profit margin.
- Higher discount levels, particularly 25% and 30%, resulted in negative aggregate profitability.
- Repeat customers represented the majority of the customer base.
- Maharashtra was the leading state by revenue and profit.
- Revenue alone does not always indicate profitability, making profit and profit margin important metrics for business decisions.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Control excessive discounting to protect profit margins.
- Review pricing and product costs in the Electronics category.
- Focus on high-margin product categories.
- Strengthen customer retention and loyalty strategies.
- Use revenue, profit, and profit margin together when evaluating products and regions.
